## Research

### Structure and Randomness in AI

Many complex objects can be split into simple, orderly, and _structured_ parts, while isolating those that are chaotic, unpredictable, or _pseudorandom_. This principle is exemplified by _regularity lemmas_ in combinatorics (Szemerédi, 1975) and complexity theory (Trevisan, Tulsiani, and Vadhan, 2009). My research applies this paradigm to questions about machine learning and artificial intelligence.

### Fairness and Alignment

As AI becomes increasingly involved in consequential decision-making, it is important to anticipate and navigate its pitfalls in the _fairness_ of its treatment of a diverse user base and the _alignment_ between its behavior and these users' interests.
